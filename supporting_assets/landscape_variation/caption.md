# Supporting Figure L1

Within-airport variation in landscape measurements

Each spatial representation includes 1,375 national airports and 15,125 airport-years from 2015–2025. Panel A shows the square root of mean within-airport sample variance. Panel B shows the median airport-specific eleven-year range. Airports receive equal weight. Both panels use percentage points and share the displayed color scale. Disc labels indicate cumulative radii; ring labels indicate nonoverlapping distance bands. The 0–3 km ring coincides with the 3 km disc. Cells show rounded values; multiscale_landscape_variation.csv retains all 36 summaries at full numerical precision. These summaries combine physical cover change and classification variation. They describe map inputs rather than independently measured ecological change or causes of prediction error.
