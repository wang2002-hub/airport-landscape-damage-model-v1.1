Annual influence under frozen hierarchical EBL predictions

Population: the eight selected target airports, 2015–2025; 5,936 eligible reports and 309 recorded damage outcomes. Each airport has eleven annual deletions. All eligible years remain in primary analyses. The national model, calibration and retained report probabilities remain fixed. These ranges describe observed annual influence; they are not confidence intervals or national-development uncertainty.

annual_influence_deletions.csv: all 88 deletions; airport is the three-letter airport code; omitted_year identifies the removed year; retained_n and retained_damage are counts after deletion; bias_pp is 100*(E-O)/N, in percentage points; oe is O/E.
annual_influence_ranges.csv: eight full-precision range summaries, with bias_min_pp/bias_max_pp and oe_min/oe_max across all eleven deletions.
annual_influence_by_year.csv: all 88 airport-year aggregates; reports and damage are counts; observed_pct and predicted_pct are percentages; bias_pp is predicted minus observed percentage. Expected counts can be recovered as reports*predicted_pct/100.
annual_influence_display.csv and annual_influence_display_definition.json preserve the complete compact display, definitions and full-period differences. Figure S7 shows every deletion and full-period estimate. Overlapping retained collections do not provide independent replications.
