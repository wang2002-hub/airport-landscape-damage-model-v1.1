Static canopy comparisons

canopy_increment_metrics.csv contains all four static-canopy comparisons at full saved precision, including base and augmented Brier/logarithmic losses, their differences, relative Brier changes and Brier interval endpoints. Negative differences favor added canopy. Column low/high applies only to the Brier difference. The display multiplies this difference and its interval by1,000; absolute losses retain original units. Differences are computed before rounding.

Each comparison uses the same11,124 national temporal test reports,669 damage outcomes and803 airports. Intervals use2,000 paired airport resamples with predictions fixed. Static2015 canopy uses3km for hierarchical regression and8km for boosting under the original settings. Annual and fixed2015 refer to the land-cover setting. The positive boosting/fixed2015 difference remains included. Intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalent performance.

canopy_increment_display.csv and canopy_increment_display_definition.json retain every compact display cell and its definitions. canopy_increment_metadata.json identifies the population, scales, units and comparison scope. FigureS11B shows all four contrasts and their intervals. The separate target_canopy_context.csv and multiscale_landscape_variation.csv remain in the canopy_context directory. Their dates, coverage and map-variation definitions are preserved there. The three13km canopy dates are contextual observations; they are not interpolated annual predictors. Product version2025.6 was released after the study period. These results do not estimate vegetation-management effects.
