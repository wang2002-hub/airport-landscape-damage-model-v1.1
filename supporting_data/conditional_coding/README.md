# Conditional coding model and transfer diagnostics

period_model_scores.csv contains all 15 population-period-reference records. The national test includes 669 damaged reports with 551 M? outcomes. All targets together include 309 damaged reports with 252 M? outcomes. Their disjoint calendar subsets contain 195/150, 37/33 and 77/69 damage/M? records. Calibrated, uncalibrated and constant predictions are evaluated on matched reports within each population. Both fitted versions share the saved national coefficients; only the calibrated version applies the national 2023 logit transformation. The constant is 236/287. All scores are descriptive fixed-prediction summaries without sampling or development intervals. No target outcomes fitted or calibrated the model.

annual_composition.csv preserves 22 population-year records, including report counts, damaged reports, documented extent, M? and annual proportions. Those proportions describe the retained database snapshot; they do not isolate changes in institutional coding. annual_prediction_diagnostics.csv retains the full annual performance diagnostics. The archived analysis_protocol.json records the exploratory protocol and prior viewing of outcomes. coding_model_fit.json retains training preprocessing, 15 coefficients, calibration parameters and optimization information. The nonintercept ridge coefficient was 0.0001, as reported in the original model specification.

damage_subtype_decomposition.csv retains all eight auxiliary fitted-coding decompositions and their counts, expectations, ratios and discrepancies. These apply a calibrated conditional coding prediction to each of 5,936 eligible target reports and multiply it by the damage probability. The target-transfer failure limits interpretation of these decompositions. They are not the empirical annual-proportion partition in manuscript Section 4.5 and Table S16. Conditional coding performance is evaluated only among damaged reports; the auxiliary product decomposition sums over all eligible reports. The original four display tables remain in the display CSVs and display_definitions.json. No result was discarded on grounds of poor performance, and no fitting or resampling was repeated. These files have not been publicly released.
