# Conditional count limits and width sensitivity

conditional_exceedances.csv preserves every count in the ten-row national summary, including both 95% and 99.8% levels for all five references. Supplementary Table S9 pairs these levels within a row and adds each reference's all-airport dispersion factor. Slashes separate ordered values; they do not denote ratios. Nominal expected-count upper bounds are 68.75 and 2.75 across 1,375 airports. Exact expected exceedances instead sum actual airport-specific tail probabilities at the discrete limits.

dispersion_sensitivity.csv retains all fifteen reference/estimation-set combinations. The estimation sets change the factor, not the evaluation population. Every factor is applied to all 1,375 national airports. national_conditional_profiles.csv contains complete per-airport counts, expectations, O/E, conditional variance, residuals, exact limits and flags for all five references. national_width_sensitivity.csv retains every airport under all three estimation sets and both levels. national_conditional_summary.csv retains the complete existing summary. These are copied records; no distributions or fits were recalculated for this catalogue.

display_coverage.csv describes the hierarchical EBL display partition. All 144 airports with E at least five and all 1,231 lower-E airports remain in the analysis. All 602 zero-damage airports appear in the full profile and in the separate figure strip without a pseudocount. The main display boundary excludes no record from national evaluation.

target_conditional_ranges.csv retains the eight displayed target count ranges. target_ranges_full.csv preserves the existing full-precision values and both levels. Targets use frozen temporal predictions, separate from national geographic outer-fold predictions. Widening transfers the national hierarchical EBL factor to their own count limits. A conditional discrepancy is not a joint development-and-year interval or a declaration that an airport is unsafe. The smooth normal–Poisson guides in Figure 2 determine no flags.

Conditional limits assume fixed probabilities and conditional independence. Width inflation is a sensitivity calculation with unestablished nominal coverage. Neither display supplies a simultaneous national confidence guarantee. display_definitions.json preserves the complete source displays and notes, and conditional_profile_metadata.json records the scopes. These files are prepared locally and have not been publicly released.
