# Environmental measurements and selected representations

landcover_codes.csv defines all nine exhaustive land-cover groups and preserves the original code mapping. Land-cover fractions share the valid land-cover-cell denominator. Imperviousness is outside this composition and is converted from percent to a proportion for fitting. The measurement_definitions.json file gives the six zones, coverage threshold, missing-coverage rules, candidate variables and time interpretation. The environmental grid contains 1,581 airport references and 17,391 airport-years before analytic outcome selection; it is distinct from the final 1,383-airport cohort.

selected_configurations.csv preserves 48 specifications across four information blocks, two model families, one temporal split and five geographic folds. The model_configurations directory preserves all 48 complete saved configurations, including training preprocessing and calibration. selected_representation_display.csv and display_definitions.json preserve the original twelve display rows. The twelve-row display lists EL and EBL choices for both model families within each evaluation partition. Its calibration intercepts and slopes apply to the logits of uncalibrated EBL probabilities; full configurations also preserve the other information blocks. selected_representation_table.json preserves the paired-family display and all explanatory notes. The source record SRC-S50 label in this file and display_definitions.json identifies the source display, while the current supplementary text points to selected_representation_display.csv. b03, b08 and b13 denote cumulative 3, 8 and 13 km discs; rings denotes the six-variable ring representation.

Disc and ring alternatives change the variables as well as their spatial support. Six disc inputs comprise water, wetland, cropland, grassland, pasture and imperviousness. The six ring inputs comprise water plus wetland and cropland in each of three rings. Differences in their performance cannot identify a purely spatial radius effect. All choices were made in the corresponding development data, not from target outcomes. Calendar-year map measurements are retrospective; their release date and reference year have different meanings. Airport-centered measurements are not collision-location habitat observations.

These files reproduce saved definitions and model selections. The measurements and environmental reconstruction component remain separate project inputs; no new rasters, predictors, fitted models or resamples were produced. Current files have not been publicly released.
