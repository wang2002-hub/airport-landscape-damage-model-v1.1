# Expanded recorded event information

The seven-field extension adds HEIGHT, DISTANCE, TIME_OF_DAY, AC_CLASS, SPEED, TYPE_ENG and NUM_ENGS to every E, EL, EB and EBL specification. These are retrospective registry fields. Their inclusion does not establish when an individual value became available to an analyst, nor validate a pre-encounter forecasting application. Landscape contrasts compare EL with E and EBL with EB within the expanded specification; they do not estimate the benefit of adding the seven fields to the primary model.

seven_field_metrics.csv preserves all sixteen national and pooled-target metric records, including AUC, O/E and signed mean probability difference. seven_field_contrasts.csv retains all four national paired Brier differences and saved percentile endpoints in original units. S54 multiplies only the differences and endpoints by 1,000. Absolute scores remain in original units. Negative differences favor landscapes. Intervals use 2,000 paired airport resamples with fitted predictions fixed, without multiplicity adjustment or repeated development. The national test has 11,124 reports and 669 damage outcomes; targets have 5,936 reports and 309 outcomes. Predictions are conditional on the retained reported-strike population, not flight exposure.

seven_field_predictions contains all eight saved prediction files, preserving event identities, outcomes, airport and year. seven_field_configurations retains all eight complete selection, preprocessing, fitting and calibration records. Height, distance and speed use log-one-plus transformation, followed by training-median imputation and training-based standardization. Speed is restricted to 0–400 indicated knots. Missingness indicators and category encodings derive from training records. NUM_ENGS is categorical in the fitted extension. Forward development years are 2019–2022, final training spans 2015–2022, calibration is 2023, and the national test spans 2024–2025. Every information block undergoes its own candidate selection. Target records do not enter training or calibration.

four_field_configurations preserves the eight exploratory models adding height, distance, time of day and aircraft class. Their records are identified by d4_enhanced in auxiliary_metrics.csv and D4 in auxiliary_contrasts.csv. These complete auxiliary files also contain distinct canopy comparisons, which belong to S9.4. Four-field and seven-field evaluations overlap in observations and are not independent replications. Results had been inspected before the expanded specifications were selected; the analysis is exploratory.

absolute_display.csv, increment_display.csv and display_definitions.json preserve every original display value. The consolidated S54 places baseline losses next to increments and retains every model and contrast; AUC and O/E remain in these support records. No new model, resample or observation was generated. Files are prepared locally and have not been publicly released.
