# Landscape increment comparisons

Differences are augmented minus baseline probability loss. Negative differences favor landscapes. CSV values retain original loss units; document display tables multiply differences and interval endpoints by 1,000. These are losses for reported strikes with known damage labels, not per-flight risk or a management intervention effect.

landscape_national_verified.csv preserves all eight national contrasts and their baseline and augmented scores, report and outcome denominators, and fixed-prediction intervals. landscape_local_verified.csv preserves 32 primary EB-to-EBL results across eight airports, two families and two periods. Its intervals concern Brier loss; paired_gains.csv additionally supplies log-loss intervals. local_display.csv retains all 32 full-period local contrasts, including E-to-EL before bird information. Table S26 selects the primary EB-to-EBL contrast consistently for every airport and both families, regardless of direction or interval width.

National fixed-prediction comparisons use 2,000 paired airport resamples. Local full-period comparisons use 2,000 paired year resamples of eleven years. Recent local comparisons span two years and are presented as points; any archived recent interval columns in paired_gains.csv are not the reported local inference. That file also retains pooled-target airport-resampling rows, which are separate comparisons and are not the single-airport year-resampling results in Table S26. Each contrast matches the same report identifiers and outcome labels within its population. Intervals are pointwise and have no multiplicity adjustment.

landscape_pipeline_intervals_verified.csv retains all ten repeated-development interval summaries. development_attempts preserves all 200 original attempt records, including calibration failures 25 and 132. The 198 successful records contain fitted-model diagnostics, selected representations and loss differences. landscape_pipeline_selected_scales.csv and landscape_pipeline_failure_inventory.json preserve selected-scale and failure inventories. National airport histories are resampled jointly across training, calibration and testing. Target collections stay fixed. Target intervals therefore concern development uncertainty alone, while the national interval also incorporates the resampled test composition. These are distinct from fixed-prediction intervals and from the joint uncertainty analysis for absolute EBL quantities in Section S6. All reported percentiles condition on successful attempts.

display_definitions.json and the three display CSVs preserve the original presentation records. No new model fit, resampling, observation, radius selection or outcome-based comparison selection was performed. Files are prepared locally and are not publicly released.
