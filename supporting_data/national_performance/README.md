# National performance and calibration

national_performance.csv retains all eighteen national evaluation rows, including the E, EL, EB and EBL information blocks for the hierarchical and boosting families, plus ordinary EB. Brier loss, log loss, AUC, average precision and O/E keep their stated populations. The compact Supplementary Table S7 places all five bird-informed references in each evaluation beside the paired loss differences.

paired_national_losses.csv preserves all eight complex-minus-ordinary comparisons with their intervals, including contrasts favoring either procedure and intervals spanning zero. Differences and limits are multiplied by 1,000. All eight comparisons use 2,000 airport resamples of matched fixed predictions. Absolute losses and differences retain their existing independent rounding; do not reconstruct paired estimates or intervals by subtracting rounded cells. EB comparisons hold the input variables constant; EBL comparisons also change information by adding landscapes.

diagnostic_calibration.csv contains all six coefficient estimates and intervals for the frozen temporal EBL predictions. Calibration-in-the-large fixes the slope at one. Joint intercept and slope are estimated together. These coefficients describe test calibration and do not update the evaluated probabilities. Their intervals use 1,000 airport-cluster resamples, distinct from the 2,000 resamples used for paired losses. Figure S2A–B retains the grouped calibration display; Panel C concerns a separate composition analysis.

The geographic evaluation contains 46,167 reports with 3,096 damage outcomes. The temporal evaluation contains 11,124 reports with 669 outcomes. Scores weight reports equally. Comparison across these populations is descriptive; paired differences compare methods within each population. Airport-equal summaries in S4.2 address a different weighting question. display_definitions.json retains the complete source display definitions, and performance_metadata.json records the units and scopes. These files are prepared locally for packaging and are not publicly released.
