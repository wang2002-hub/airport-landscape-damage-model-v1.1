# Outcome and map sensitivity comparisons

S56 reports every scenario's primary EB-to-EBL Brier increment for national temporal-test reports and pooled targets. Each paired row puts national values above target values. Absolute EBL loss retains original units; displayed increments and endpoints are multiplied by 1,000. Negative differences favor landscapes. Population and outcome denominators accompany every row. O/E, log loss and the event-only E-to-EL contrast remain in the complete source tables. national_display.csv and pooled_target_display.csv preserve all original display records.

sensitivity_performance.csv contains 320 rows across eight scenarios, four blocks, the national test, pooled targets and eight individual targets. sensitivity_paired.csv contains 160 paired rows covering E-to-EL and EB-to-EBL. These complete files include Brier and log-loss differences and saved interval endpoints. Pooled intervals resample airports with fixed predictions; individual-target intervals resample years. These are pointwise intervals without multiplicity adjustment and do not repeat development. They differ from Section S6 joint development-and-year intervals. Limits printed as 0.000 in archived displays retain limited precision; full-precision values govern interpretation.

predictions and configurations preserve all32 saved model outputs and complete preprocessing/calibration records. All four hierarchical blocks are refitted within each scenario, with fixed settings and 3 km landscape inputs where applicable. The M? restriction removes known damage reports of undetermined extent from development and evaluation. Its reduced-prevalence population is distinct from the full known-label collection. Its O/E uses probabilities from the restricted refit, not frozen all-damage probabilities. This is an auxiliary fixed-setting analysis and should not replace the matched coding analysis in Section S8. Completeness thresholds act on airport-year known, nonconflicting outcome fractions among eligible near-airport reports, not on predicted risk.

The contemporaneous, one-year-lagged and fixed-2015 map scenarios share identical eligible 2016–2025 records across training, calibration, national test and targets. Complete environmental predictors and adequate source-map coverage determine their intersection. Annual change-rate fields are excluded from this eligibility rule. National tests cover 2024–2025; target collections include every eligible year in the scenario. These are retrospective map comparisons, not evidence of real-time product availability. The ILR specification replaces the composition with eight coordinates for nine cover groups, retains imperviousness separately and floors zero shares at0.000001 before renormalization. Spatial exclusion removes national airports within50km of a target from all temporal stages. target_distance_audit.csv preserves the distance screen. Static-canopy comparisons belong to S9.5 and are unchanged.

All results are exploratory. The consolidated table selects the same primary contrast in every scenario, regardless of sign or interval. Favorable event-only results remain documented in the text and full source records. No model fit, resample or observation was added in this consolidation. Files are local preparation materials and have not been publicly released.
