# Recorded consequences and data dimensions

consequence_completeness.csv preserves all20 original records: national and pooled-target populations plus eight target airports, each for all eligible reports and for damage reports. These populations cover2015–2025. National and target groups are disjoint; individual-target rows are nested within the target total. Engine, flight-effect, downtime and cost fields may coexist in one report, so column counts must not be added as distinct events. An affirmative engine field denotes a recorded damage flag. Nonaffirmative fields do not establish that an engine was inspected or undamaged. EFFECT availability is not equivalent to an adverse flight effect. flight_effect_counts.csv preserves39 category records, including explicit None and missing fields.

recorded_amounts.csv preserves eleven observed-data columns from the retained eight-airport summary. Its n column counts all eligible reports; damage is the denominator for consequence availability. The projection omits model-expected counts and discrepancies to keep observed amounts separate. Source values are unchanged. Available and missing downtime and repair counts sum to damage reports. AOS measures aircraft hours out of service; COST_REPAIRS and COST_OTHER are nominal reported US dollars. All available numerical consequence values in the damage subsets are positive. Missing values are not zeros. Invalid, zero and positive categories remain separate in consequence_completeness.csv. Medians depend only on available values. Recorded hours sums are neither complete downtime burden nor staff work. Nominal repair amounts are not pooled across years into an inflation-adjusted economic total. No avoided loss, model-attributable harm or hypothetical staff-hour conversion is estimated.

source_inventory_snapshot.csv preserves388 saved source entries totaling102209816781 bytes. This is a historical source inventory, not a fresh filesystem survey or a current release count. Decimal GB equals one billion bytes. It includes overlapping and auxiliary files and cannot establish the marginal cost of landscape predictors. source_inventory_summary.json preserves the recorded dimensions. The national environmental panel has1375 airports and15125 airport-years. Three discs with six measurements yield272250 values; six ring terms yield90750. These are table entries, not independent reports, raster-pixel counts or hours of work. The derived dimensions do not measure staff effort, acquisition prices, maintenance or economic benefit.

The three original display tables remain in their display CSVs and display_definitions.json. S62 retains all eight airport amount/missingness rows. The detailed consequence-count and data-dimension displays are supplied here alongside their sources. All results are descriptive; no model fit, bootstrap, imputation or new observation was added. These files are prepared locally and have not been publicly released.
