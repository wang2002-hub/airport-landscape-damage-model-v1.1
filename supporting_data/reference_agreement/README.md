# National reference ordering and selected-set agreement

full_population_spearman_matrix.csv preserves the full-population Spearman matrix. rank_concordance.csv gives all ten model pairs for each of the complete 1,375-airport population and the common 144-airport subset. It includes Spearman and Kendall correlations, upper/lower decile overlap and lower-group sizes. Boundary ties remain included. The full-population lower groups consist of the same 602 zero-damage airports. ordering_display.csv and exceedance_overlap_display.csv preserve all original displayed cells; display_definitions.json preserves their notes.

exceedance_set_overlap.csv contains 80 rows: ten pairs, two populations and four conditional/width specifications. Each row gives both set sizes, intersection, union and Jaccard overlap. Parentheses in exceedance_overlap_display.csv contain the TWO SET SIZES in pair order, not intersection/union. Figure S8 instead labels intersections and unions explicitly. The common subset selects airports using hierarchical EBL E at least five and applies those IDs to every reference. Factor estimation sets are distinct from evaluation populations. Every flagged airport lies in the common subset, which explains equal exceedance-set summaries between populations.

national_reference_profiles.csv preserves all 6,875 airport-reference rows and exact conditional flags. national_width_sensitivity.csv preserves all airports under three factor estimation sets and both saved limit levels. common_subset_membership.csv records all 720 model-airport combinations, highest-decile cutoffs and flags. common_subset_pairwise.csv gives all ten pairs, including top-set sizes and overlaps. ordinary_hierarchical_profiles.csv supplies Figure 4A's 144 paired values. full_population_spearman_matrix.csv preserves the existing matrix file.

These files are copies of saved results. No fit, probability distribution or resampling was repeated. Descriptive agreement does not measure matched probability loss, certify individual ranks or establish airport safety equivalence. Widened limits are sensitivity calculations with unestablished nominal coverage. Perfect overlap among singleton sets describes one common airport. The files have not been publicly released.
