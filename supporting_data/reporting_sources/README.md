# Reporting channels reporter categories and composition

SOURCE is the report channel/type; PERSON is the reporter category. Multiple denotes multiple report types for one strike, not multiple reporters. The two fields partition the same records and must not be summed as separate populations. Missing field values remain in denominators. reporting_category_composition.csv has 960 category records; reporting_category_by_year.csv has 5,264 annual records. reporting_population_audit.csv defines 30 population panels and missingness counts. Every national population excludes all eight targets.

Before environmental screening, national known-label and unresolved-label populations contain 48,983 and 8,995 reports; target counts are 5,936 and 1,340. These are different populations. Full-period final SOURCE has zero missing values. PERSON is missing for 206 national and 16 target reports. Complete categories include empty and sparse groups. Original displays for S39, S40 and S41 are preserved in their CSV files and display_definitions.json.

Category discrepancies do not estimate reporting probabilities or assign responsibility to reporter groups.
