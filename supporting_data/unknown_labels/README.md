# Unknown damage labels and descriptive bounds

The near-airport collection has 54,919 known-label and 10,335 unresolved-label reports. Environmental or coordinate exclusions remove 2,816 known-label reports and 196 recorded damage outcomes, leaving 52,103 reports and 3,405 damage outcomes. All eight target known-label populations pass this screen. Target totals are 5,936 known and 1,340 unresolved labels among 7,276 near-airport reports. M? is known positive damage with undetermined extent; it is not a missing binary label.

selection_bridge.csv is the normalized population summary. selection_bridge_source.csv preserves the exact source. The source unknown_label row has damage=0 from aggregation of an unobserved outcome; this is not evidence of zero damage. The normalized file leaves this single cell blank and adds an explicit damage_status field. All other source cells remain unchanged. No event label was modified. Airport-row damage counts are recorded positives among known-label reports only.

missing_label_bounds.csv contains all eight count-based lower/upper bounds. Lower bounds assume unresolved outcomes are undamaged and upper bounds assume they are damaged. These are descriptive limits for the expanded report collection, not confidence intervals, model-performance bounds or incidence among unreported strikes. The file also preserves auxiliary fixed-proportion threshold columns. Their known_reference_mean concerns known-label reports only, so they are not valid O/E tipping points for the expanded population. Negative auxiliary thresholds are infeasible. These fields are not presented as substantive results.

unknown_label_scenarios.csv preserves 24 scenario rows. The field named assumed_unknown_to_known_risk_ratio is an assumed ratio of damage proportions within reported events, not a flight-incidence risk ratio. Each combined rate uses the original counts and min(1, ratio times the known-label damage proportion). Missing outcomes remain unknown. target_label_completeness.csv preserves eight exact fractions; Table S24 combines their rounded percentages with the original bounds. All five original displays, including airport names, scenarios and quality indicators, remain in the display CSVs and display_definitions.json.

label_information_quality.csv preserves the two full-population quality summaries; annual_label_information_quality.csv preserves 22 annual summaries; airport_year_label_information.csv preserves all 6,518 airport-year records. Generic bird descriptions are text proxies rather than validated identification. Recorded affirmative remains fields do not imply negative values elsewhere. No fitted model or resampling was run. These files have not been publicly released.
