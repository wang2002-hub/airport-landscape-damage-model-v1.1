# Weighting report volume and case mix

airport_equal_scores.csv contains all 24 equal-airport score rows across the national and selected-target evaluations. It reports one equally weighted mean score per airport. Its Bias column uses predicted minus observed probability in percentage points. It does not contain report-weighted results. National report-weighted scores are available in national_performance.csv, and hierarchical_ebl_weighting.csv preserves both national weightings for hierarchical EBL.

report_volume_display.csv preserves all twelve rows of Table S8. report_volume_calibration.csv retains full-precision observed and predicted proportions, marginal intervals, paired gap intervals and Brier losses under both weighting schemes. report_volume_membership.csv retains all 1,375 geographic-evaluation and 803 temporal-evaluation airport records, including zero-damage airports. A shared airport can have a row in each evaluation. Membership and quantile boundaries remain fixed. Two thousand within-group airport resamples supply pointwise intervals conditional on fixed predictions. Both observed and predicted quantities use the same sampled airports. Group gap intervals are paired, whereas figure bars are marginal.

Positive volume gaps mean observed damage exceeds prediction. This sign is opposite to Bias in airport_equal_scores.csv. Full-precision gap files use probabilities, while Table S8 uses percentage points. The evaluation populations and report-count ranges differ and overlap across years; agreement is descriptive rather than independent replication. Small report count does not identify airport size or flight exposure.

case_mix_standardization.csv contains all eight airports with explicit supported, total and unsupported report counts. Unsupported counts are total minus supported counts. All proportions retain their source precision, with no inferred damage counts. standardization_display.csv and display_definitions.json preserve the original complete displays. National strata combine phase, aircraft mass class and bird size and require at least 20 reports from five airports. All targets are excluded from the national source. Supported target proportions and reweighting use the same reports; all-target proportions have a broader denominator when unsupported reports exist. The descriptive exercise has no confidence intervals or causal-adjustment interpretation and leaves fitted predictions unchanged.

These files are local packaging materials and have not been publicly released. weighting_metadata.json records units, sign conventions, population overlap and interval scope.
